# GAMEPLAY Guide

## Stage Structure
In **Stay-offline**, the game is divided into several stages, each building upon the last. Players navigate various environments, each with unique themes and challenges. The progression through each stage is linear, yet players must explore to uncover hidden elements.

## Challenge Types
The game features a variety of challenges, including:
- **Time Trials**: Complete tasks within a time limit.
- **Puzzle Challenges**: Solve intricate puzzles to unlock new areas.
- **Combat Challenges**: Engage in battles with adversaries to gain rewards.

## Riddles
Players will encounter riddles that test their wits, such as:
- **"I speak without a mouth and hear without ears. I have no body, but I come alive with the wind."** (Answer: Echo)

## Obstacles
Expect to find numerous obstacles, including:
- **Trapdoors**: Hidden floors that trigger if players step on them.
- **Moving Platforms**: Platforms that require timing and precision to navigate safely.

## Trials Explanation
Each trial encompasses multiple segments:
1. **Trial of Strength**: Test physical capabilities.
2. **Trial of Wisdom**: Solve puzzles and answer riddles.
3. **Trial of Endurance**: Survive against relentless enemies.

## Tips for Players
- **Explore Thoroughly**: Always check your surroundings for hidden items.
- **Use Your Resources Wisely**: Conserve health and power-ups for tougher challenges.
- **Team Up**: If possible, work with other players to tackle difficult sections.

## Story Teaser
In a world where technology reigns supreme, one must learn to disconnect and rediscover the ancient ways. Embark on a journey to unveil the mysteries of the past while battling new-age challenges.
